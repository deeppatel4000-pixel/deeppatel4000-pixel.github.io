Experience

Most of my time outside class goes to BAPS, a Hindu organization with a mandir in Lowell. I have volunteered with BAPS Charities since 2017, led youth programs at our mandir in Lowell since 2022, and spent four months in 2023 working full time on CNC fabrication at BAPS Akshardham in New Jersey. Work and school activities follow.

Youth leadership

Weekly assemblies, a summer camp, and a Diwali program.

Youth Activities Coordinator, BAPS Bal Mandal

Lowell, MA · August 2022 to present

Every week I run Bal Mandal, the children's assembly at our mandir, for more than 70 kids with a team of 15 peer teachers. I plan and teach lessons on Hinduism and on the values and habits the kids can carry with them as they grow, and I use discussion and one-on-one mentoring to keep the younger students involved.

Bal Balika Summer Shibir

I help lead the annual Bal Balika Summer Shibir, a three-day camp for children in grades 4 through 8 that teaches religious values through activities, stage programs, and breakout sessions. More than 1,000 participants take part. On the 2023, 2024, and 2025 shibirs I put together the stage program and coordinated between the food service, hall setup, stage, and activity teams.

Kids Diwali Celebration

I also help lead the Kids Diwali Celebration at our mandir, where 400 to 500 children come each year for a stage program, activities, food, and arcade-style games built around Diwali and the values taught at the mandir. I helped lead it in 2023, 2024, and 2025, and I plan to continue in 2026.

Fabrication

Four months of full-time CNC work.

Full-Time Volunteer, BAPS Akshardham

Robbinsville, NJ · July 2023 to October 2023

I worked full time in the digital fabrication shop for a large cultural building project. I drew architectural components in CorelDRAW and Adobe Illustrator, turned those drawings into CNC-ready files, then ran the industrial CNC routers and hot-wire cutters that cut the parts from wood, PVC, and foam. I worked with senior designers and other volunteers to keep every piece accurate, finished on time, and cut without wasting material.
