Projects

These are the two projects I have taken the furthest. ORBIX is an aerospace engineering web app that I conceived, researched, and directed. The bell nozzle started as a propulsion design and took me through CAD, CFD, debugging, and fabrication. Each case study shows the work behind it, including what went wrong.

01

Software · Aerospace web application · 2026

ORBIX

Thirteen engineering calculators, a mission planner, and profiles of five aircraft and five launch vehicles. A Verification page checks the results against published worked examples, and 26 of 36 compared values fall within rounding. The idea and research are mine, and AI coding assistants wrote the code under my direction.

02

Engineering · Self-directed propulsion project · April 2026

Bell Nozzle

A bell nozzle drawn in OpenSCAD from a six-point profile, meshed and run through SimFlow and OpenFOAM, and printed in PLA. The simulation never converged, so most of the project became working out why. The case study shows that result as it happened.
