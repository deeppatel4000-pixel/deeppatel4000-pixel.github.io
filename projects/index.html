ORBIX

The site pairs 13 calculators and a mission planner with profiles of ten real vehicles, five aircraft and five launch vehicles. Its answers are checked against published worked examples on a Verification page, where 26 of 36 values match to within rounding. ORBIX was my idea and my research, and I built it with AI coding tools.

Engineering · Self-directed propulsion project · Since September 2025

Bell Nozzle

I drew this nozzle in OpenSCAD from a six-point profile, meshed it, ran it in SimFlow and OpenFOAM, and printed it in PLA. The CFD never reached a converged solution, and trying to track down the cause became the bulk of the work.
