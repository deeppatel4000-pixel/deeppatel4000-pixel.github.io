01

Software · Aerospace engineering web app · 2026

ORBIX

ORBIX has thirteen engineering calculators, a mission planner, and profiles of five aircraft and five launch vehicles. A Verification page runs selected calculations against published worked examples: 26 of 36 values match within rounding, and the other 10 stay on the page with the reason for each. I did the research and planned each tool, and used AI coding tools to write the code.

02

Engineering · Self-directed propulsion project · Since September 2025

Bell Nozzle

I tried designing a bell nozzle because I wanted to understand how one really works. I sized it with NASA CEA, drew the profile in OpenSCAD, and printed the part in PLA, and the printed geometry matches the area ratio CEA called for. The CFD in SimFlow never converged. My logged runs went from aborting at iteration 2 to reaching iteration 24, and the case study and the 10-page lab report cover what I tried, what I think went wrong, and what I would change.
