Work

01

Software · Aerospace engineering web app · 2026

ORBIX

Thirteen engineering calculators, a mission planner, and five aircraft and five launch vehicles. Drag an orbit and the delta-v updates as you move it. A Verification page checks results against published worked examples. The idea, research, and decisions are mine, and AI coding assistants wrote the code under my direction.

02

Engineering · Self-directed propulsion project · April 2026

Bell Nozzle

I wanted to know how bell nozzles actually work, so I tried to design one. I drew the profile in OpenSCAD, ran the flow volume through SimFlow and OpenFOAM, and printed the part in PLA. The simulation never converged. The best run reached iteration 24, so most of the project turned into figuring out why.
