02

Engineering · Self-directed propulsion project · Since September 2025

Bell Nozzle

I tried designing a bell nozzle because I wanted to understand how one really works. After drawing the profile in OpenSCAD, I ran its flow volume through SimFlow and OpenFOAM and printed the part in PLA. The CFD never converged. My best run got to iteration 24, and most of the case study is about why. The 10-page lab report includes the full NASA CEA output.
