01

Software · Aerospace engineering web app · 2026

ORBIX

An aerospace engineering web app built around real aircraft and launch vehicles. I did the research and planned each tool, and used AI coding tools to write the code.

  • 13 calculators and a mission planner, from the rocket equation to oblique shocks
  • 10 real vehicles, five aircraft and five launch vehicles, with sourced specs
  • 26 of 36 checked values match published examples within rounding. The other 10 stay on the page with the reason for each.

02

Engineering · Self-directed propulsion project · Since September 2025

Bell Nozzle

I wanted to know how a rocket nozzle works, so I designed one.

  • Sized it with NASA CEA for a LOX/RP-1 engine at 100 bar
  • Drew it in OpenSCAD and printed it in PLA. The part matches the area ratio CEA called for.
  • Set up the CFD in SimFlow. It never converged. My best run reached iteration 24, and the case study and 10-page lab report show what I tried.
